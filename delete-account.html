# Delete Your WireWhat Account *Host this at a public URL (e.g. `https://wirewhat.live/delete-account.html`) and set it as the "Delete account URL" in Google Play Console → App content → Data safety.* ## How to delete your account 1. Open the WireWhat app. 2. Tap the menu (⋮) from the mode selector screen. 3. Tap **Close account**. 4. Confirm when prompted. This permanently deletes your account. You do not need to contact support to do this. ## What gets deleted When you close your account, we permanently delete from our servers: - Your registration record (name, phone number, phone lookup index) - Your publisher profile, if you activated one - Replay metadata associated with your account ## What is retained - **YouTube replays** you published before closing your account are not automatically removed, since they are hosted on YouTube rather than our servers. Contact **support@wishlandtechnology.com** if you need a published replay taken down. - Data may be retained briefly in backups or logs for operational/security purposes before full removal. ## No account, no app required You do not need to keep the app installed to request deletion — if you are unable to sign in, email **support@wishlandtechnology.com** from the phone number or account details associated with your account and we will delete your data on request.
