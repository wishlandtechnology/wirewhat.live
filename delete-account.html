Delete Your WireWhat Account

This page explains how you can permanently delete your WireWhat account and what happens to your data after deletion.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the WireWhat app.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) from the mode selector screen.
  3. Select Close Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
Important: Closing your account permanently deletes your account from WireWhat. This action cannot be undone.

What Gets Deleted

When your account is closed, we permanently delete the following information from our servers:

  • Your registration record (name, phone number, and phone lookup index).
  • Your publisher profile, if you created one.
  • Replay metadata associated with your account.

What Is Retained

  • YouTube replays published before your account is closed are not automatically removed because they are hosted on YouTube rather than WireWhat servers.
  • If you would like a published replay removed, please contact us at support@wishlandtechnology.com.
  • Certain operational logs or backup data may be retained for a limited period for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or disaster recovery before being permanently deleted.

If You Cannot Access the App

You do not need to keep the app installed to request account deletion.

If you are unable to sign in, simply email support@wishlandtechnology.com with the phone number or account details associated with your WireWhat account, and we will process your deletion request.
