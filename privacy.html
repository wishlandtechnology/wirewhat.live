WireWhat Live Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WireWhat Live, a product of Wishland Technology Private Limited, collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our services.

Data We Collect and Why

Phone Number

Used for one-time verification (OTP) when you sign in. We do not sell your phone number or share it for marketing purposes.

Name and General Location

Your name and general location (city or area entered during registration) are used to identify you in chat and help match viewers with nearby publishers.

Precise Location (GPS)

Used to match viewers with nearby publishers, notify viewers when you go live, show publishers how far they are from an incoming request, and attach approximate location to uploaded replays. Location is sent to our servers while you have the app open and are using these features (requesting a stream, waiting for requests as a publisher, going live, or uploading a replay). **Location is collected only while the app is open and in use; WireWhat does not access your location in the background.** When the app is closed, matching uses the last location you shared while it was open. The app explains this and asks before Android's location permission prompt appears.

Camera and Microphone

Used when you broadcast live or record a video within the app. Live video and audio are streamed through our infrastructure and delivered to viewers who join the session. Recorded videos may be processed on our servers and may be published to WireWhat Live's official YouTube channel for replay viewing.

On-device face detection (live broadcasts)

When "Blur faces" is enabled for a live broadcast (on by default), the app detects faces directly on your device and blurs them in the video before it is streamed out, to help protect the privacy of bystanders. This detection runs entirely on-device — no image data is sent anywhere for this purpose. It is best-effort, not a guarantee: fast motion or brief detection gaps can leave a face unblurred for a moment. Recorded replay uploads go through a separate, additional server-side face-blur pass (see below)

Optional Stream Descriptions and Chat Messages

Shared with the other party during a live session to coordinate help.

Device Token (Push Notifications)

Used to notify publishers of incoming requests and viewers when a broadcaster goes live nearby.

Live Streams vs Recorded Replays

Live Streams

Live streams are delivered in real time. We do not permanently store the live video on our servers after the session ends, unless you opt in to save the stream to YouTube.

Recorded Replays

you upload are stored temporarily for processing (watermark, publish) and then published to our YouTube channel for others to watch. Title, description, and location may be shown to nearby viewers searching for replays.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Verify user identity and account ownership.
  • Match viewers and publishers.
  • Deliver live video, audio, and chat services.
  • Send push notifications.
  • Process and publish optional video replays.
  • Improve platform performance, reliability, and security.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate the service, including cloud hosting providers, SMS providers for OTP verification, mapping services, YouTube replay publishing, analytics tools, or when required by law.

Location data is collected only to enable location-based features within the service and is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Your Choices

  • You may decline location, camera, or microphone permissions, although some features may not function properly.
  • You may sign out of the application at any time.
  • Close your account from the mode selector menu (⋮ → Close account) to permanently delete your registration, phone index, publisher profile, and replay metadata stored on our servers. You will need to register again to use the app.
  • YouTube replays already published before account closure are not automatically removed from YouTube. Contact support if you need a replay taken down.
  • To report safety, privacy, or abuse issues during a live session, use **Report** on the player or incoming-request screen, or **About → Report a problem**

Account and Data Deletion

Safety

WireWhat does not verify other users. You are responsible for your safety and for respecting others' privacy. Do not share sensitive personal information in chat or on camera. Use the app only for lawful purposes and only in places you are allowed to observe or record.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page and through links provided in the application and app store listings.
